**Branch:** cmame-paper. **Status:** r4 DRAFT (14 October 2026), not stamped; the CONFIG_SHA256 line below is open. Stamping fills it before the run (Sec. 7); post-stamp criterion changes are prohibited.
**Authority:** this English file governs. **Publication:** every outcome below is reported whatever it is; the manuscript submitted to Computer Methods in Applied Mechanics and Engineering on 11 October 2026 (`paper/cmame/`, tag `cmame-submission`) is not changed by this run, and any revision of it reports every claim's verdict.

## 1. Questions
The manuscript's comparisons were read on the validation instances of its two corpora, the same instances on which the runs were monitored, and in two dimensions the instances on which the results that motivated the comparison had been seen (its Sec. 9, Limitations). Each corpus also holds instances on which no recorded run or tool trained a network, computed a label or evaluated a network (Sec. 2). This run evaluates the stored states of the manuscript's networks, unchanged, on those instances, and re-reads eight of the manuscript's claims there by rules fixed in advance (Sec. 5). It answers one question per claim: does the statement, as the manuscript makes it about its validation instances, also hold on instances of the same distribution that played no part in any training, selection or verdict?

It is a test of the trained networks on new instances, not of training: the states are those whose values the manuscript reports, no network is trained, and the seeds are the same three. It tests whether the validation instances were representative of their distribution for the statements read on them, among them statements chosen after the runs (T1, T2, T3, H2c and D2); it cannot show what other seeds, other training runs, other corpora or other budgets would give (Sec. 5, What a verdict licenses; Sec. 9).

Notation as in PREREG_CM2D: an instance has a stiffness matrix K, four load vectors F_j and reference solutions U*_j; a prediction u_j has relative energy gap g_j = ||u_j - U*_j||_K^2 / ||U*_j||_K^2; a load case is worse than the zero field when g_j > 1, which holds exactly when Pi_h(u_j) = u_j^T K u_j / 2 - F_j^T u_j > 0 (the manuscript's Corollary "Zero-field test", its Sec. 3.5). The frozen suite computes the gap from the energies, g_j = (Pi_h(u_j) - Pi_h(U*_j)) / |Pi_h(U*_j)|, so that g_j - 1 = Pi_h(u_j) / |Pi_h(U*_j)|: here the sign identity holds by computation.

## 4. Evaluation and measurements
For every state and every holdout instance, the frozen metric suite (`fejepa.metrics.evaluate_fields`, as every run evaluated): relative energy gap, relative L2 displacement error, relative L2 von Mises error over the elements, peak von Mises error, critical-region recall, each the mean over the instance's four load cases; and per load case the relative energy gap g_j and Pi_h(u_j). A seed's value of a metric is its mean over the set's instances; a row's value is the mean of its three seeds' values. Nothing else is evaluated, apart from the content check of Sec. 3, which records the same quantities on the validation instances.

## 5. Claims and rules
Each claim is the manuscript's own statement about its validation instances, here read on the holdout set; each is **REPLICATED** or **NOT REPLICATED** by the rule below, or **NOT EVALUATED** when a state it reads failed its content check or a value it reads is not finite, except in the claims H1, H2a, H2b and H2c and the reading H3, which follow PREREG_CM2D Sec. 4: a non-finite per-seed value of the reference row leaves the claim NOT EVALUATED, one of the new row alone is a divergence and the claim is NOT REPLICATED (H3: "the stiffness-norm transformer diverged"). The two rules differ on purpose: in T1, T2, T3 and D2 a non-finite value of either row leaves the claim NOT EVALUATED, also when it is the row the claim says is better, while H1 to H2c read a divergence of the new row as CM2D's claims did. Eight claims, eight separate verdicts, no multiplicity correction. "Better" is lower, except for critical-region recall, where it is higher; a tie is not better. Applied to the validation arrays of the two reports (and the per-load gaps of `records/cmame/timing/fields/energies_val.npz` and `records/cmame/spectra/export/spectra_val.npz`), every rule below reads REPLICATED, with the values the manuscript quotes (`tests/test_holdout_rules.py`).

Three dimensions (h3d):
- **T1.** Abstract: "a transformer trained on the energy alone was more accurate on every metric, in each of three seeds, than the same network trained on the solutions of the same instances"; Sec. 5.1: "on all five metrics, in the seed mean and in each of the three seeds". REPLICATED iff, for each of the five metrics, the label-free transformer is better than the supervised transformer (`labels`) in each seed (seed s against seed s) and in the seed mean (20 comparisons). The manuscript's statement also covers the supervised transformer with the energy term, which has no stored state (Sec. 3).
- **T2.** Abstract: "a supervised graph network had the smaller displacement error on most instances and the larger von Mises error on almost all, a post hoc reading"; Sec. 5.2: lower displacement error on 84.4% to 92.6% of the instances in each seed, higher von Mises error on 767 of the 768 instance-seed pairs. REPLICATED iff the graph network's displacement error is lower than the label-free transformer's on more than 50% of the instances in each seed, and its von Mises error higher on at least 95% of the instance-seed pairs. "Most" is read as a strict majority, the weakest reading of the word; "almost all" as at least 95%; both are fixed without any holdout value (on the validation instances 84.4% to 92.6% and 99.9%).
- **T3.** Abstract: "Every supervised network was worse than the zero field on some load cases, all flagged by the sign of the energy" (Sec. 5.3, measured after the run: 126 load cases of the supervised transformer and 106 of the graph network, of 3,072 each). REPLICATED iff each of the two supervised networks with stored states (`labels`, `mgn`) has at least one load case, over its three seeds, with g_j > 1, and Pi_h(u_j) > 0 holds on exactly those of its load cases (load cases within 1e-9 of g_j = 1 are left out of the sign check). The sign part is an identity of the computation (Sec. 1): T3 can fail only through the count, which on the validation instances was 4.1% and 3.5% of the load cases; a REPLICATED verdict carries little evidence beyond the counts, which are the information (Sec. 6). The supervised transformer with the energy term has no stored state (Sec. 3).

Two dimensions (h2d), with the noise guard of PREREG_CM2D Sec. 4: for a new row A and a reference B with three per-seed values each, rel = mean(A)/mean(B) - 1, SE_rel = sqrt(s_A^2/3 + s_B^2/3) / mean(B) with sample standard deviations, tau = max(10%, 2 x SE_rel); "lower beyond the guard" means rel < -tau (`fejepa.analysis.adjudicate_w9.guard`).
- **H1.** CM2D's H1 (Sec. 6.1: 71.3% lower): REPLICATED iff the label-free transformer's relative energy gap is lower than L_D's beyond the guard.
- **H2a.** CM2D's H2a (78.2% lower): REPLICATED iff L_K's relative energy gap is lower than L_D's beyond the guard.
- **H2b.** CM2D's H2b (54.9% lower): REPLICATED iff L_K's von Mises error is lower than L_D's beyond the guard.
- **H2c.** Abstract: "the counterpart more than halved its von Mises error, supporting a pre-registered hypothesis"; Sec. 6.2: "lowered the von Mises error by more than half" (the seed means' ratio 0.451, rel = -54.9%; per seed 0.514, 0.330 and 0.524, so on the validation instances only the seed mean is halved). REPLICATED iff L_K's seed-mean von Mises error is less than half of L_D's: rel < -50%, the point value, without a guard.
- **D2.** Abstract: "In two dimensions the label-free transformer had a larger displacement error than its supervised counterpart but smaller errors on every other metric"; Sec. 6.1: "on every metric but the displacement error, in each of the three seeds", its displacement error "higher in each seed" (seed means 0.0634 against 0.0505). REPLICATED iff the label-free transformer's displacement error is higher than L_D's and each of its other four metrics better, in each seed (seed s against seed s) and in the seed mean (20 comparisons).
- **H3** (a reading, no verdict). CM2D's H3: L_K (A) against the label-free transformer (B), relative energy gap, reported as exactly one of: the stiffness-norm transformer lower beyond the guard; the label-free transformer lower beyond the guard; no difference shown (within the guard); the stiffness-norm transformer diverged; or not evaluated. CM2D read "no difference shown"; the reading with the roles exchanged is reported beside it.

What a verdict licenses. REPLICATED: on these 686 or 4,111 instances, with these states, the statement holds as the rule reads it. NOT REPLICATED: it does not hold as the rule reads it on these instances; the manuscript's statement must then be qualified in any revision, with these numbers beside the original ones. Neither verdict says anything about other seeds, other training runs or other corpora. The rules were applied before the stamp to resamples of the validation instances of the holdout sets' sizes (`scripts/holdout_resample.py`, `records/cmame/holdout/resample.json`: 1,000 resamples per scheme, the same instances for every row and seed, the seeds kept). Drawn from the validation instances directly, every claim read REPLICATED in all 1,000 resamples. With the validation set's own sampling added (256 instances drawn from the validation instances first, then the set from those), every claim read REPLICATED in all 1,000 except D2, in 969 of 1,000: each of the other 31 failed through the peak von Mises error in seed 0 (0.128 against 0.133 on the validation instances). If the validation instances are representative, every verdict is therefore expected to read REPLICATED. No resample of either scheme read NOT REPLICATED for T1, T2, T3, H1, H2a, H2b or H2c (H2c came closest, its largest rel -50.7%), so that a NOT REPLICATED of one of these would be unlikely to be a matter of which instances were drawn; a NOT REPLICATED of D2 can be one of sampling (31 of 1,000 resamples). The run tests that representativeness, not seed-to-seed or training variation.

## 7. Execution mechanics (exact)
- Stamping, after approval: the sandbox is scanned again (`scripts/holdout_scan_sets.py`, the same directories), and the stamp is not made unless the scan exits 0, that is, unless every set of the corpora's generators and seed is within the bounds of Sec. 2; the scan's time and result are recorded with the stamp (BRANCH_NOTES). `scripts/stamp_prereg_holdout.py --date "<D Month YYYY>"` refuses a file whose status line, CONFIG_SHA256 line or last line is not this r4 draft's, and a configuration that does not load (`fejepa.analysis.holdout.load_spec`); it fills CONFIG_SHA256[holdout_v1] with the configuration's canonical SHA-256 (as `fejepa prereg --stamp`), the status line with the date, and the last line with this file's SHA-256, computed over the file with that line reading `PREREG_HOLDOUT_SHA256 = <record after commit>`; then it reads the result back and verifies it. Commit; tag `prereg-holdout`, an annotated tag (no other tag on that commit); push the branch and the tag; the remote tag is verified before the run (`git ls-remote`, RUNBOOK_CMAME Sec. E).
