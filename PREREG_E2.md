**Branch:** wp8-lejepa. **Status:** r10 STAMPED 29 September 2026 on wp8-lejepa `cc9a168` + this stamp commit (tag `prereg-e2`), after the bench of Sec. 3 returned (records `records/wp8/bench_e2_m512.json`, `records/wp8/bench_e2_m1024.json`); hashes in the footer. Post-stamp criterion changes are prohibited. **Authority:** this English file governs. **Publication:** GO and NO-GO are both publishable; the deliverable either way is the measured accuracy/cost frontier.

## 1. Question
Can a token-bottleneck surrogate (farthest-point-seeded aggregation of the mesh into M tokens, attention over the M tokens, per-node conditional decoding from a continuous blend of the node's k = 6 nearest tokens -- their latents and relative-position embeddings -- and the node embedding) reach parity with the per-node FE-JEPA transformer in the energy norm under the SAME label-free AR objective, while reducing the fine-scale training step time by an order of magnitude? The energy anchor is unchanged: the decoded displacement feeds the same exactly assembled discrete potential energy.

**Decoder (decided by the PI on 29 September 2026, before the bench):** continuous. Each node blends its k = 6 nearest tokens (`model.decode_k = 6`) with the compactly supported quadratic weights w_j proportional to (1 - d_j / R)_+^2, d_j the distance to seed j and R the distance to the 7th nearest seed, normalised to sum 1: a token's weight vanishes exactly where it leaves the node's six nearest and R is continuous, so the decoder's conditioning is a continuous function of position. Kernel and k were fixed at the node scale (r10), before any bench: the narrowest blend of this family that, across the mesh edges of both bench meshes at both M, changes no more often by more than 1.0 or 1.5 (L1 of the weight vector) than the piecewise-linear (Delaunay, barycentric) interpolation of the same seeds -- in-band M = 512: 18.9% / 1.1% of edges (linear 27.0% / 3.7%); in-band M = 1024: 42.7% / 6.0% (52.6% / 9.9%); fine M = 512: 2.2% / 0.1% (5.1% / 0.8%); fine M = 1024: 9.9% / 0.3% (13.6% / 1.8%); on average 2.7 tokens effectively contribute per node (linear 2.5), and the weight drawn across cavities is no larger than under the hard assignment. The r9 draft's Franke-Little weights over 4 tokens were continuous but concentrated on the nearest seed (32% of the in-band M = 1024 edges still changed by more than 1.5), and the r8 hard nearest-token decoder -- 47% / 59% of the in-band and 32% / 41% of the fine edges joining nodes of different tokens -- is the k = 1 case; neither is an arm. The encoder's pooling is unchanged (scatter-mean of node embeddings over the nearest-seed cells; the nearest seed by exact distance, identical to the earlier assignment up to floating-point ties). The bench of Sec. 3 times this decoder.

## 4. Kills and GO
- **K1 (accuracy; decided by the PI on 29 September 2026, before stamping):** at either M, for each of the in-band energy gap (AR cell, pool 1024) and the fine zero-shot displacement error, compare SEED MEANS (the Phase-2 aggregation rule); K1 fires if, for either quantity, the relative change of the bottleneck's seed mean over the baseline's exceeds max(10%, 2 x SE_rel), where SE_rel = sqrt(s_base^2/3 + s_M^2/3) / mean_base with s the seed-to-seed SAMPLE standard deviations (n - 1) => the bottleneck is retired as a Phase-3 candidate at that M. A non-finite per-seed value of the bottleneck is a divergence and counts as K1 firing. The thresholds actually applied are reported with the verdict as the achieved resolution. Why: the baseline's seed spread is large (energy gap 0.0104 / 0.0105 / 0.0065, CV 25%; fine zero-shot 0.281 / 0.287 / 0.207, CV 18%); the r6 seed-median 10% rule killed an exactly equivalent architecture in 53% of simulated trials. Properties of this rule by simulation (200,000 trials, 3 seeds per arm): false kill of an equivalent architecture about 11%; median effective band 37% (energy gap) and 27% (fine displacement); a true 50% degradation is caught with probability about 0.56 (energy gap) and 0.78 (fine displacement). A GO therefore means no degradation detectable at that resolution -- not parity at 10% -- and is reported as such.
- **K2 (speed):** set-up-free fine-scale step time not below 2.0 s => no speed case at that M.
- **GO:** no K1 AND set-up-free fine step time below 1.0 s at that M; the verdict states the achieved K1 resolution.
- Adjudication: `scripts/adjudicate_e2.py --base-report runs/phase2/report_phase2b.json`; it refuses a baseline whose configuration SHA-256 is not the Phase-2b stamp, an E2 report that is not a stamped, guard-verified run of this file, an E2 report of the wrong M, reports that differ beyond the architecture (corpus manifests, split, seeds, schedule including `e8.ar_lr`, dims), non-finite per-seed values, an E2 report whose decoder is not `decode_k = 6`, and a bench without a valid set-up-free fine phase at that M with that decoder, measured on CUDA outside smoke mode with the protocol of Sec. 3 (pairs of 10 / 110 steps, three pairs, `phase2b_v1.json`, TF32; a missing phase is refused, not read as K2); the verdict echoes the speed lines and the bench phase it used.

## 5. Execution mechanics (exact)
- Configurations are generated, not hand-edited (`scripts/make_e_series_configs.py` from `configs/phase2b_v1.json`; it writes `model.decode_k = 6`): `experiments.e8.ar_only = true` (no supervised cells, no naive rows, label-dependent kills marked unevaluated), `experiments.p3_transfer` in zero-shot-only form (`fewshot_budgets: []`, `naive_budget: 0`), `e6`/`wp6`/`e1` disabled (the runner refuses otherwise: they probe the FE-JEPA interface); both validate under `run-config --dry-run` (model kind, plan, zero pool-label need, guard status).
- Economy: the runs buy no labels (validation and fine-evaluation labels exist from Phase 2); each solve ledger reads 0 and the corpus manifests stay as recorded above; any other value is a deviation to explain.
- Stamping: one labelled line per configuration (below), filled by `fejepa prereg configs/e2_m<M>.json --stamp --prereg-file PREREG_E2.md`; the guard refuses both arms until both lines are stamped. Then commit, tag `prereg-e2`, push, and verify the remote tag before any GPU time. This file's SHA-256 (last line) is recorded in the stamp commit itself, computed over the file with that line reading `PREREG_E2_SHA256 = <record after commit>` (a file cannot contain its own hash).
- Runs: one `run-config` per M on the box with `PYTORCH_CUDA_ALLOC_CONF=expandable_segments:True` and `--label-workers 1` (RUNBOOK_E_SERIES Sec. 2c); `--reuse-states` only for a restart.
