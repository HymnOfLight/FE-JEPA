# PRE-REGISTRATION E2 -- token bottleneck with continuous decoding

**Branch:** wp8-lejepa. **Status:** DRAFT r7, 29 September 2026; stamps only after the bench of Sec. 3 is on record. **Authority:** this English file governs. **Publication:** GO and NO-GO are both publishable; the deliverable either way is the measured accuracy/cost frontier.

## 1. Question
Can a token-bottleneck surrogate (farthest-point-seeded aggregation of the mesh into M tokens, attention over the M tokens, per-node conditional decoding from the token latent, the node embedding and the relative position) reach parity with the per-node FE-JEPA transformer in the energy norm under the SAME label-free AR objective, while reducing the fine-scale training step time by an order of magnitude? The energy anchor is unchanged: the decoded displacement feeds the same exactly assembled discrete potential energy.

## 2. Arms
- **FE-JEPA AR** (baseline, read, not retrained): the AR cells of the Phase-2b report `runs/phase2/report_phase2b.json` (file SHA-256 `320b6db5060ecae9f4747327228c7705d30877bece67d09af2f68d6c466f2794`; configuration `316f5e6e282db9c11509d8d1d2ed54d229ded9d6367c63b3a13abd7709ba4899`, PREREG_PHASE2B r2): in-band validation displacement 0.0312 / 0.0319 / 0.0267, energy gap 0.0104 / 0.0105 / 0.0065 (seed mean 0.0091); fine zero-shot displacement 0.281 / 0.287 / 0.207 (seed mean 0.258). The Phase-2 report is NOT a baseline: its AR cells are the D14 instrument defect.
- **Bottleneck AR, M = 512** (`configs/e2_m512.json`) and **M = 1024** (`configs/e2_m1024.json`): `model.kind = bottleneck`, `model.n_tokens = M`; everything else is the Phase-2b configuration byte for byte (dims 256/8/8, features, corpora `runs/data3d_phase2` manifest `dc75628290bb...` and `runs/data3d_phase2_fine` manifest `0f859ad98a01...`, split 256/seed 1, the same 1,024-instance training prefix, 200 epochs at 1e-3, seeds 0/1/2, TF32 fp32) -- a test pins this.

## 3. Measurements
- In-band validation displacement error and relative energy gap per seed (E8 AR cells), and the fine-scale zero-shot values per seed on the Phase-2b fine evaluation set (256 instances, labels already on disk).
- Step time and peak memory at the largest in-band bench instance (12,318 nodes) and at the fine bench instance (41,367 nodes): `scripts/bench_phase2_preconditions.py configs/phase2b_v1.json --bottleneck-tokens M`, phases `bottleneck<M>_inband_0` and `bottleneck<M>_fine`. The step time is set-up-free (differential: two runs of n1 and n2 steps after a warm-up, (T2 - T1)/(n2 - n1)); the set-up-inclusive number is recorded beside it. Reference: the FE-JEPA transformer's fine step is 14.7 s under the current code (per-block activation checkpointing, R17; 12.1 s before it).

## 4. Kills and GO
- **K1 (accuracy; decided by the PI on 29 September 2026, before stamping):** at either M, for each of the in-band energy gap (AR cell, pool 1024) and the fine zero-shot displacement error, compare SEED MEANS (the Phase-2 aggregation rule); K1 fires if, for either quantity, the relative change of the bottleneck's seed mean over the baseline's exceeds max(10%, 2 x SE_rel), where SE_rel = sqrt(s_base^2/3 + s_M^2/3) / mean_base with s the seed-to-seed SAMPLE standard deviations (n - 1) => the bottleneck is retired as a Phase-3 candidate at that M. The thresholds actually applied are reported with the verdict as the achieved resolution. Why: the baseline's seed spread is large (energy gap 0.0104 / 0.0105 / 0.0065, CV 25%; fine zero-shot 0.281 / 0.287 / 0.207, CV 18%); the r6 seed-median 10% rule killed an exactly equivalent architecture in 53% of simulated trials. Properties of this rule by simulation (200,000 trials, 3 seeds per arm): false kill of an equivalent architecture about 11%; median effective band 37% (energy gap) and 27% (fine displacement); a true 50% degradation is caught with probability about 0.56 (energy gap) and 0.78 (fine displacement). A GO therefore means no degradation detectable at that resolution -- not parity at 10% -- and is reported as such.
- **K2 (speed):** set-up-free fine-scale step time not below 2.0 s => no speed case at that M.
- **GO:** no K1 AND set-up-free fine step time below 1.0 s at that M; the verdict states the achieved K1 resolution.
- Adjudication: `scripts/adjudicate_e2.py --base-report runs/phase2/report_phase2b.json`; it refuses a baseline whose configuration SHA-256 is not the Phase-2b stamp, an E2 report of the wrong M, reports that differ beyond the architecture (corpus manifests, split, seeds, schedule, dims), and a bench without set-up-free timing.

## 5. Execution mechanics (exact)
- Configurations are generated, not hand-edited (`scripts/make_e_series_configs.py` from `configs/phase2b_v1.json`): `experiments.e8.ar_only = true` (no supervised cells, no naive rows, label-dependent kills marked unevaluated), `experiments.p3_transfer` in zero-shot-only form (`fewshot_budgets: []`, `naive_budget: 0`), `e6`/`wp6`/`e1` disabled (the runner refuses otherwise: they probe the FE-JEPA interface); both validate under `run-config --dry-run` (model kind, plan, zero pool-label need, guard status).
- Economy: the runs buy no labels (validation and fine-evaluation labels exist from Phase 2); each solve ledger reads 0 and the corpus manifests stay as recorded above; any other value is a deviation to explain.
- Stamping: one labelled line per configuration (below), filled by `fejepa prereg configs/e2_m<M>.json --stamp --prereg-file PREREG_E2.md`; the guard refuses both arms until both lines are stamped. Then commit, record this file's blob SHA-256, tag `prereg-e2`, push, and verify the remote tag before any GPU time.
- Runs: one `run-config` per M on the box with `PYTORCH_CUDA_ALLOC_CONF=expandable_segments:True` and `--label-workers 1` (RUNBOOK_E_SERIES Sec. 2c); `--reuse-states` only for a restart.
