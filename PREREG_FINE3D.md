# PRE-REGISTRATION FINE3D -- the three-dimensional fine-mesh failure: a mesh-independent scale on the token bottleneck (R2a) and label-free fine-tuning at the fine mesh (R3)

**Branch:** wp9-pool. **Status:** r1 DRAFT (15 October 2026), not stamped; the three CONFIG_SHA256 lines below are open. Stamping fills them all at once, before any run (Sec. 8); post-stamp criterion changes are prohibited. **Authority:** this English file governs. **Publication:** every outcome below is publishable; any write-up reports every verdict of Sec. 6 with its rel, tau and per-seed values, R3's fine displacement reading beside R3-E and R3-V (Sec. 6), and the R2b recommendation as Sec. 6 computes it.

## 1. Questions
In three dimensions the label-free (AR) transformer of the Phase-2b run loses accuracy on the fine mesh it never trained on: on P3's fine evaluation set (lc 0.0374; about 36,000 nodes per instance, median; 15,175 to 71,749) its displacement error is 8.62 times its in-band error (seed means 0.258 and 0.0300; the manuscript's Sec. 7). wp8's post-hoc reading 4e found an amplitude deficit in it: the energy-optimal amplitude c* (label-free; its "median" here and below is the mean over seeds of each seed's median over the instances and load cases) is 1.23 on the fine set against 1.00 in band, and scaling each load case by its c* lowers the fine displacement error by 68%. wp9 (PREREG_W9, H2 SUPPORTED) cut the analogous growth in two dimensions with S, a decode scale and load features that do not change with the mesh: the F5 displacement error fell by 76.0%, and its ratio to the in-band error by 74.1% (4.67 to 1.21).
- **Q-R2a.** Does S reduce the growth of the displacement error from the training mesh sizes (lc 0.0579-0.0906) to the fine mesh in three dimensions? Asked of the token bottleneck at M = 1,024 tokens (PREREG_E2's arm; retired by E2's K1 as a Phase-3 candidate, but trained in 2.5 h per seed against the transformer's 27.3 h). Its amplitude deficit is larger than the transformer's: its median c* on the fine set is 1.74 / 2.06 / 1.50 by seed (1.00 in band), and c* lowers its fine error by 78%; its growth is smaller -- the fine error is 6.43 times the in-band one (seed means 0.574 and 0.0892) -- because its in-band error is three times the transformer's. R2a is a mechanism test: it decides, by the rule of Sec. 6, whether S is worth running on the transformer (R2b: three seeds of 27.3 h, with its own pre-registration).
- **Q-R3.** Starting from the Phase-2b AR states, does fine-tuning with the AR objective -- the energy alone, no reference solution -- on 16 fine instances lower the fine-mesh energy gap and von Mises error below what the labelled fine-tuning on the same 16 instances reached? P3's labelled few-shot fine-tuning at budget 16 brought the displacement error down to 0.0531 (seed mean; 1.8 times the in-band 0.0300) but not the energy gap (0.0702, 7.7 times the in-band 0.0091) or the von Mises error (0.166, 3.0 times 0.0554). The manuscript (Sec. 7, 9) states that label-free adaptation at the fine mesh was not tested.

## 2. R2a: arms
| Configuration | Seeds | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| `configs/r2a_b_m1024.json` | 3-5 | E2's M = 1,024 arm trained again on fresh seeds (`e8.seed_offset = 3`): S's reference. |
| `configs/r2a_s_m1024.json` | 3-5 | As `r2a_b_m1024` with S: `model.decode_scale = "l1"` with `model.decode_scale_factor = 2^-10` (the decoded field is multiplied by 2^-10 times the sum of the absolute values of the battery's nodal force components instead of by the largest of them) and `model.features.load_density = true` (per-node load densities -- each nodal force divided by the boundary area (traction) or the volume (body load) the node carries -- and a load summary of mesh-free ratios). |

Both: everything else is E2's configuration (`configs/e2_m1024.json`, CONFIG_SHA256 `526a5688...` in PREREG_E2): the corpus `runs/data3d_phase2` (manifest `dc75628290bb...`) and its fine set `runs/data3d_phase2_fine` (`0f859ad98a01...`), split 256 / seed 1, the 1,024-instance training prefix, 200 epochs at lr 1e-3 (204,800 AR steps per seed), dims 256 / 8 / 8, M = 1,024 tokens, `decode_k = 6`, TF32 with fp32 weights, one worker; E8 AR only, P3 zero-shot only (no labels bought). The configurations are generated (`scripts/make_fine3d_configs.py`, which refuses E2's file unless its canonical SHA-256 is PREREG_E2's stamped line); a test regenerates them byte for byte and checks that each differs from E2's only in `_comment`, `out`, `prereg_file`, `e8.seed_offset` and, for S, its three model keys. The top-level `seeds` key of E2's file stays as it is: the runner reads the seeds from `experiments.e8`.

Why fresh seeds: E2's own states were read (wp8 4e) and their fine-mesh deficit is why R2a exists; comparing S with them would select on their training noise (PREREG_W9 Sec. 2's argument). E2's seeds 0-2 serve instead as a coarse check of the training path on the box (Sec. 7). The selection on the instances remains: R2a reads the validation split and fine set on which 4e found the deficit, and S's gain should be largest where the deficit is; this favours support, as PREREG_W9 Sec. 6 states for H2.

The factor 2^-10 only sets the level of S's decoded output, by the rule that gave wp9's 1/64: the power of two nearest the median of max|F| / sum|F| on the training family. On 256 instances drawn afresh from the training family's sampler (`scripts/fine3d_scale_factor.py`, generator seed 93000, which no corpus or set has used; assembly only; `records/wp9/fine3d/scale_factor_3d.json`) the median is 0.00113 (10th-90th percentile 0.00068-0.00190, i.e. 0.69-1.95 times 2^-10) and grows with lc (rank correlation 0.62), so at training mesh sizes S asks the network for outputs of about E2's level on the median instance (1.16 times). E2's corpus itself was not read for this, so that its tail -- the 720 instances after the training pool's 1,024, of which R1 (branch cmame-paper, PREREG_HOLDOUT) evaluates 686 -- stays unread. Meshed again at the in-band ends and middle and at the fine lc (16 geometries, the wp8 post-hoc remesh geometries), max|F| at lc 0.0374 is 0.186 times its lc 0.0906 value (median; range 0.168-0.210; lc itself 0.413 times), and sum|F| is unchanged to within 0.07%: the bottleneck's scale shrinks roughly with the face area per node, S's does not. (These medians equal, to the last digit, the ones the box recorded for the same geometries in wp8's post-hoc pass, `records/wp8/posthoc/amp_e2_m1024.json`: the sandbox's gmsh meshed them as the box's did.)

S changes the decode scale and the load inputs together, and R2a tests them together; a SUPPORTED R2a is a statement about S as a whole. The code: the bottleneck takes `decode_scale`, `decode_scale_factor` and `features.load_density` with the FE-JEPA transformer's validation and values (`fejepa.models.features.check_decode_scale`, `decode_scale_value`, `build_features_battery`); the token geometry (seeds, blend weights) does not depend on them; with the defaults the bottleneck is E2's bit for bit (Sec. 9). P3 now takes E8's seed offset: its zero-shot rows read the states E8 trained (`ar_p1024_s3/4/5.pt`) and record those seeds; without an offset it reads seeds 0..n-1 as before, and an offset written into P3's own block is refused.

## 3. R3: arm
`configs/r3_eft_b16.json`, run by `scripts/r3_energy_finetune.py`: for each seed 0, 1, 2, the Phase-2b AR state (`runs/phase2/e8_states/ar_p1024_s<seed>.pt`, SHA-256 as the Phase-2b report records them: `a7b5c71c...`, `e9c0c400...`, `e0f2210b...`; the report itself `records/wp8/e2/baseline/report_phase2b.json`, SHA-256 `320b6db5...`, as PREREG_E2 Sec. 2 records it), loaded strictly into the report's own model, is fine-tuned with the AR objective (energy only, `AR_CONFIG`) on the first 16 instances of the fine few-shot prefix (`instance_00256` to `instance_00271` of `runs/data3d_phase2_fine`: the instances on which P3's labelled fine-tuning at budget 16 trained) with that fine-tuning's schedule (50 epochs, 800 steps; lr 1.5e-3, read from the Phase-2b configuration; AdamW with weight decay 1e-4; gradient clip 1.0; 5% linear warm-up, then cosine) and seed. Both trainers draw each epoch's instance order from the seed, so the energy fine-tuning visits the 16 instances in the labelled fine-tuning's order (a test checks it through the script; each result records the seed it trained with and the SHA-256 of the epoch orders that seed determines); the training instances' labels are dropped when they are loaded.
- Reference: the labelled fine-tuning at 16 in the Phase-2b report (P3, `fewshot["16"]["finetune"]`), per seed: displacement 0.0559 / 0.0500 / 0.0533, energy gap 0.0776 / 0.0758 / 0.0573, von Mises 0.1732 / 0.1766 / 0.1468. By design the only difference is the objective: the energy instead of the displacement labels (64 fine solves, 16 instances x 4 load cases, which R3 does not read). The reference is not run again: it is the Phase-2b record (run 22-28 September 2026 at `prereg-phase2b-r2` with the supervised trainer), and R3 runs on `prereg-fine3d` with the label-free trainer, possibly on another host and GPU driver. The two share the optimiser, the schedule, the clipping, each seed's starting state and instance order; both training paths are bitwise unchanged on CPU since the Phase-2b code (the regressions of Sec. 9); on CUDA any two runs differ at round-off. That record is also the one whose energy gap and von Mises error motivated R3 (Sec. 1), which matters for the rule's properties (Sec. 6).
- Before each seed trains, a content check: the state's predictions on the first 8 fine evaluation instances and the first 8 in-band validation instances must reproduce the report's per-instance displacement errors and energy gaps (P3's zero-shot cell, E8's AR cell) to a relative 1e-3 (wp8's post-hoc pass reproduced them exactly; a wrong state or code path deviates by orders of magnitude more); otherwise the script stops before training.
- Budget 16 only (the PI's choice of 14 October 2026; the budget of 64 would cost about 13 h per seed).

## 5. Measurements
- **R2a.** Per seed: E8's AR cell on the validation split; P3's zero-shot pass on the fine set and again on the validation split; the frozen metric suite per instance (relative energy gap, displacement relative L2, von Mises relative L2, peak von Mises error, critical-region recall). Per-seed values are means over a set's instances; arm values are seed means. Secondary readings only.
- **R3.** Per seed, after fine-tuning: the same metric suite and the amplitude readings (c* per load case, c_b, errors after scaling) on the fine set and the validation split.

## 6. Hypotheses and verdicts
Noise guard (the E-series rule, PREREG_E1 r13): for arm values A (new) and B (reference) from three seeds each, rel = mean(A)/mean(B) - 1 and SE_rel = sqrt(s_A^2/3 + s_B^2/3) / mean(B) with sample standard deviations (n - 1); tau = max(10%, 2 x SE_rel). "Lower beyond the guard" means rel < -tau; "worse beyond the guard" means rel > tau. The tau applied is reported with each verdict, as are the per-seed values.
- **R2a (primary).** Reference: `r2a_b_m1024`. **SUPPORTED** iff all of: (i) S's fine displacement error is lower beyond the guard; (ii) S's fine / in-band displacement-error ratio is lower beyond the guard (the growth itself shrinks, not only both levels); (iii) K1, read on E8's in-band cell: neither S's energy gap nor its displacement error there is worse beyond the guard. Otherwise **NOT SUPPORTED**, with the failing conditions named. A non-finite per-seed value of S fails the condition it enters (reported as diverged); a non-finite value of the fresh baseline voids the comparison (R2a not evaluated). Properties by simulation (`scripts/fine3d_sims.py`, `records/wp9/fine3d/simulations.json`: 200,000 trials per case, three log-normal seed values per arm, the spreads of E2's run -- coefficients of variation 10% and 11% for the fine and the in-band displacement errors, 20% for the in-band energy gap -- for both arms, a seed's values drawn independently): a support without a true effect occurs in 1.4% of trials (1.7% if S's seeds spread 1.5 times as much); a true fine reduction of 20% / 30% / 50% / 70% with the in-band errors unchanged is supported in 38% / 70% / 89% / 90% of trials. The support rate stays near 90% however large the effect, because K1 reads two in-band metrics at the guard's resolution and fires on noise in about one trial in ten (the E-series' own false-kill rate). If S also lowers the in-band errors by 10%, condition (ii) needs more at small effects (21% / 54% / 97% / 98%); if it raises them by 10%, K1 fails more often (37% / 58% / 65% / 65%). These rates rest on three seeds' spreads: with both arms' spreads twice E2's, a true reduction of 30% / 50% / 70% is supported in 25% / 66% / 87% of trials, and with the spreads of E2's M = 512 arm (18%, 48% and 65%) in 11% / 33% / 64%, the rate without an effect staying at or below 1.4%; a positive correlation within a seed between its fine and in-band errors (which three seeds cannot estimate) lowers that rate and raises the others (rho 0.5: 0.8% without an effect; 52% / 85% / 90% / 90%). A NOT SUPPORTED R2a with an observed fine reduction of 30-50% is therefore weak evidence against S.
- **The R2b rule (fixed here, before R2a runs).** R2b -- the paper's transformer with S, three seeds -- is **recommended** iff R2a is SUPPORTED and S's fine displacement error is at most half the fresh baseline's (rel <= -50% in condition (i)). Otherwise it is not recommended on this evidence. A recommendation, not a verdict: the PI decides, and R2b gets its own pre-registration. Why half: the oracle amplitude c* lowers the bottleneck's fine error by 78% and the transformer's by 68% (Sec. 1); a run of several days on the transformer is worth it if S, which does not see the solution, removes at least half of the bottleneck's fine error, where its deficit is the larger one; the recommendation thus rests on the model in which S has the most to correct. By the simulation above, a true fine reduction of 50% / 70% leads to a recommendation in 45% / 90% of trials with the in-band errors unchanged (49% / 98% if S also lowers them by 10%; 32% / 65% if it raises them by 10%), one of 30% in under 0.01%.
- **R3-E (primary).** Reference: the labelled fine-tuning at 16. **SUPPORTED** iff the energy-fine-tuned networks' fine relative energy gap is lower beyond the guard; otherwise **NOT SUPPORTED** (reported as "worse" if worse beyond the guard; as diverged if a per-seed value is non-finite).
- **R3-V (primary).** As R3-E, for the fine von Mises relative L2 error.
- Properties of R3-E and R3-V by simulation (the same script). The reference is one recorded run, which shares each seed's starting state and instance order with the new arm, and whose spread alone fixes tau at 18.4% or more for R3-E and 11.4% or more for R3-V. Held at its record, with the energy-fine-tuned seeds spread like the reference's (coefficients of variation 16% for the energy gap, 10% for the von Mises error), a support without a true effect occurs in 0.6% of trials (R3-E and R3-V) if the record equals its expectation, in 2.2% and 4.6% if it lies 5% above it, and in 6.1% and 18% if it lies 10% above it (about a 1.0- and 1.6-standard-error event for its mean); if the energy-fine-tuned seeds spread by 50% (the zero-shot states' fine energy gaps spread by 72%: 0.530 / 0.296 / 0.091), in 12-19% (R3-E) and 15-23% (R3-V): with a skewed spread and three seeds the guard's standard error is itself noisy. A true reduction of 20% / 30% / 50% is supported in 40% / 84% / 99.99% of trials (R3-E) and 85% / 99.8% / 100% (R3-V) with the record at its expectation. Drawn afresh in every trial instead, as for a concurrent reference, the rates without an effect are 5.4% (R3-E) and 4.3% (R3-V), 7.0% and 6.0% at 1.5 times the spread, and 13% and 15% at 50%.
- **What R3-E and R3-V license.** R3-E's metric is the energy-norm error that the energy objective minimises on its training instances, and the von Mises error measures the stress that norm weights, as the displacement error is the labelled objective: they test whether the energy objective's advantage carries to the 256 held-out fine instances at the labelled fine-tuning's schedule (which was not tuned for it), not whether label-free fine-tuning is the more accurate overall. Any write-up reports the fine displacement error against the labelled fine-tuning (Sec. 7, with the guard and its direction) beside R3-E and R3-V.
- Three verdicts, no multiplicity correction: they are separate claims, each reported with its own threshold. R3-E and R3-V read the same networks and are not independent evidence. A part whose reports the adjudication refuses (Sec. 8) is NOT EVALUATED, recorded with the reason; the other part is still adjudicated.
- **What a verdict licenses.** SUPPORTED: on these seeds and instances, at this budget, model and training family, the new arm is lower than the reference beyond tau (for R2a, each of its conditions holds). NOT SUPPORTED: no such reduction was shown; it is not evidence that the effect is absent or small. A reduction smaller than tau cannot be shown by design, and one near tau is missed about half the time. No verdict is restated as "no effect"; each is reported with rel, SE_rel, tau and the per-seed values. R2a is a statement about the token bottleneck: whether S does the same for the transformer is R2b's question.

## 7. Secondary readings (reported, no verdict; exploratory)
- **R2a.** Beside each condition: the same guard on per-seed medians over the instances (for the ratio, the per-seed ratio of the medians); rel with a Welch 95% interval; a 95% interval for rel from resampling the instances (2,000 resamples, the same instances for both arms and every seed, the seeds kept). Every metric on the fine set, on P3's in-band pass and on E8's cell, S against the fresh baseline with the guard computed. Mechanism, from the post-hoc records: the median c* on both sets (near 1 on the fine set if S removes the deficit), the errors after scaling by c*, and on the remesh geometries, by lc, the predicted energy norm relative to the coarsest mesh (the bottleneck's fell to 0.41-0.58 of its coarsest-mesh value at lc 0.0374 in wp8 4e; S's should not fall) and c_b (the bottleneck's rose from 0.93-0.95 at lc 0.0906 to 1.69-2.37 at 0.0374; S's should stay near 1). If the fresh baseline does not show the deficit -- its fine / in-band ratio of seed means below 1.5 or its median c* on the fine set below 1.10, PREREG_W9's rule-1 lines -- a NOT SUPPORTED R2a is reported as uninformative about S's mechanism; the verdict stands (without the baseline's amplitude record, the c* part is reported as not evaluated). The fresh baseline beside E2's seeds 0-2 (the fine displacement error, E8's in-band energy gap and displacement error, with the guard): a difference beyond the guard is reported as a flag on the training path and changes nothing.
- **R3.** The fine displacement error, peak von Mises error and critical-region recall against the labelled fine-tuning (the displacement error is the labelled arm's own objective and is expected to favour it); every metric against the zero-shot states (the report's P3 cells); the in-band metrics after fine-tuning against E8's AR cell before it (what the fine-mesh training costs in band; the labelled fine-tuning has no in-band counterpart: P3 evaluated it on the fine set only, and its states were not kept); the fine / in-band displacement ratio after fine-tuning; c* and c_b on both sets; per seed, the paired relative difference to the labelled arm of the same seed (the reading that uses the shared starting state and instance order); beside R3-E and R3-V, the guard on per-seed medians, the Welch interval and the instance-resampling interval (the same 256 fine instances in both arms).
- Everything per seed as well as seed-averaged.

## 8. Execution mechanics (exact)
- **Stamping**, before any run: `scripts/stamp_prereg_fine3d.py --date "<date>"` fills the three CONFIG_SHA256 lines (each the canonical SHA-256 of its configuration, as `fejepa prereg --stamp` computes it), the status line and the last line, this file's SHA-256, computed over the file with that line reading `PREREG_FINE3D_SHA256 = <record after commit>` (a file cannot contain its own hash). It refuses a file that is not this draft byte for byte, configurations other than the generator's, and a `src` that is not the tree the cross-branch regression ran (Sec. 9). Then commit; run the full suite on that commit; tag it `prereg-fine3d` (an annotated tag); push the branch and the tag; the remote tag is verified before any GPU time. The branch is frozen until the session has returned: before the session and before every step, the box checks that `git describe --always --dirty --tags` -- the string every report records -- prints exactly `prereg-fine3d` and that it is the only tag on the commit.
- **The session** (`RUNBOOK_FINE3D.md`; `scripts/fine3d_session.sh` runs the steps in this order and keeps every attempt's log): `run-config configs/r2a_b_m1024.json`, `run-config configs/r2a_s_m1024.json`, the post-hoc amplitude pass on each, then `scripts/r3_energy_finetune.py configs/r3_eft_b16.json`. Every step is guard-verified against this file: a run-config refuses to start unless its configuration's hash is its own line here; R3's script likewise. A step whose output is complete is skipped when the script runs again; an R2a run with states but no report restarts with `--reuse-states` (D9; recorded in its report); R3 resumes an interrupted seed from its epoch checkpoint if this code and configuration wrote it (otherwise that seed trains from the start), does not run again a seed finished by this code and configuration (marking it as taken from disk) and runs again a seed whose result file is unusable.
- **Adjudication:** `scripts/adjudicate_fine3d.py`, from the return. It refuses an R2a report that is not a guard-verified run of this file under its own line, ran on another commit than `prereg-fine3d`, differs from E2's configuration beyond the keys of Sec. 2 or is not the stamped arm for its role, ran on another corpus or other seeds, or does not hold one evaluation per seed with a value for each of the set's instances (E8's and P3's cells); it refuses an R3 report that is not a guard-verified run of this file under its line, ran on another commit (the report's or a seed's), started from another source report or another state, failed its content check, trained with another seed, on other instances or for another number of epochs or steps (counted from the trainer's history), or lacks a seed's evaluation; and it refuses inputs whose SHA-256 is not the records' (the Phase-2b report, E2's report). A report that is missing from the return or cannot be read leaves its part NOT EVALUATED. A post-hoc amplitude record of another report, or an incomplete one, is not read (its readings are not evaluated). Restarts, resumed seeds, seeds taken from disk, repeated attempts (from the session's status file), a solve ledger above 0, and a different torch, GPU or TF32 setting between the two R2a arms are recorded as deviations; a different setting between R3 and its reference is recorded beside R3. The verdict file records the adjudicating code -- its `git describe` and its files' SHA-256 -- and whether those files and the pre-registration it read are the tag's; a difference, or a tag that cannot be read there, is a deviation.
