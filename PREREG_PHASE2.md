## 0. Scope and inheritance

Phase 2 tests the label-free exactness claim at engineering scale: unstructured gmsh tetrahedral corpora, 10k-30k dof training band, 100k dof resolution transfer. It inherits branch A (E2 verdict, frozen 31 July 2026) and the 2D-phase retirements (Sec. 1). It makes NO representation-collapse claims and runs NO JEPA arm: the E3 evidence is single-cell-thin (deviation D5) and the 2D finding "fine-tuning hurts" (raw AR dominated every fine-tuned variant) removes the pretrain-finetune pipeline from the deciding battery. The WP2 sweep's recommended mask fraction 0.2 is recorded here for any future JEPA-3D work; it is not exercised.

Engineering state inherited as defaults, all measured and committed on branch wp7-3d (head e60b3a8, version 2.1.5+wp7.4, suite 147 pass / 0 skip):
- Scale-equivariant decode (P0.5) ON. The 2D scale-blindness floor (D7) is repaired by construction; equivariance is suite-tested. No blindness arm runs in Phase 2.
- torch.compile OFF (r10 honesty correction: the production pipeline invokes the model through methods, never `__call__`, so the wrapper is a no-op -- verified on the box by empty dynamo counters; the earlier 1.46x measurement belonged to a different harness; proper integration is post-gate engineering). The bench-measured eager numbers are therefore the true baseline.
- Precision: fp32 baseline; fp16 AMP stays OFF (trajectory deviation 1.69e-2 on record; the runner refuses it). bf16 is admitted CONDITIONALLY under the pre-declared transparency pilot (L5): own miniature corpus (gmsh3d, n = 128, seed 123, production lc band, its own ledger, zero contact with the frozen corpora); arms per seed s in {0, 1}: AR pretrain (50 epochs on 96 unlabelled) and supervised+balanced anchor (50 epochs on 64 labelled), each at fp32 AND bf16 with identical seeds, evaluated on the same 32 labelled instances; acceptance, fixed here: every run finite AND, for every (arm, seed), the relative deviation of BOTH val displacement error and val energy gap between precisions <= 1e-2, AND the engagement probe confirms the encoder computes in bf16 under autocast (anti-no-op clause: output dtype is inadmissible evidence, since the mask/scale tail type-promotes to fp32 by design; without this clause a silent no-op would pass the deviation test trivially -- the compile lesson applied). PASS => the stamped config sets runtime.precision = bf16 and this file records the measurement; FAIL => fp32 stands. In either precision the energy anchor computes in fp32 by construction (autocast self-protection, suite-tested): the exact objective is never approximated. Wall-clock adjudication on record: honest per-arm projection at eager fp32 is 333-361 h; a bf16 PASS divides by the pilot's measured speedup (D8: measured, not manual).
- Encoder/decoder dims identical to the 2D deciding configuration for comparability: width 256, depth 8, heads 8; MGN comparator 256 x 8; pretraining 200 epochs @ 1e-3 (AR objective); supervised arms 200 epochs @ 1.5e-3. ## 2. Gate G2 (GO to Phase 3 / paper-grade Phase-2 claims) iff (a) AND ((b) OR (c)):

- **(a) repaired sanity (P1):** the anchored network achieves >= 3x over the zero predictor on displacement AND beats every repaired naive baseline on displacement (the naive set: knn_field and the scale-aware polynomial), at every budget.
- **(b) label efficiency (P1):** zero-label AR displacement error is within +10% of the 1,024-label supervised arm AND the AR relative energy-gap advantage over supervised is >= 40% at every budget. (This operationalises the proposal's ">= 5x label efficiency": matching a 1,024-label arm with zero labels at a 64-label decision budget exceeds 5x by construction; the thresholds are the measured-2D-calibrated directional form of C1/K2.)
- **(c) resolution transfer (P3):** the in-band-trained zero-label AR model, evaluated zero-shot on the 100k-dof transfer set, achieves displacement error <= 1.25x its in-band validation error AND beats every naive baseline (same naive set) evaluated at the fine resolution, each in its strongest form: built from the full in-band prefix (b = 1,024) and transported or fitted cross-resolution. The transfer ratio here and in KP4 is the ratio of seed-mean displacement errors. G2 = (a) AND ((b) OR (c)). Any unmet or unevaluated condition reads false; G2 false = NO-GO, and the negative branch (Sec. 10) is written. Transfer is scored on three zones: <= 1.25x is a gate-grade win; (1.25x, 1.5x] is reported as weakened transfer without gate credit; > 1.5x triggers KP4. The thresholds' calibration is in the companion note (2D-measured parity gap +3.6% against the 10% band; K2's inherited 30% band deliberately tightened; 2D cross-resolution degradation measured under 5% as the prior behind 1.25x/1.5x). One calibration caveat is frozen with eyes open: the 2D parity gap was measured under the common scale-blindness floor, which compresses relative displacement differences, and Phase 2 runs floor-repaired (P0.5 ON), so the gap may widen mechanically -- floor-decomposition of the 2D record puts the learnable-component gap between roughly +7% and +12% depending on the composition model. The 10% band is kept regardless: post-repair, parity should mean parity, and a gap above 10% is a genuine verdict for KP1, not a calibration artifact.

## 4. Kill conditions (frozen; all directional)

- **KP1** (P1): labels@1024 beats zero-label AR on displacement by MORE than 10% => the parity claim is retired at 3D scale.
- **KP2** (P1): AR energy-gap advantage over supervised < 40% at ANY budget => the energy-advantage claim is retired.
- **KP3** (P2, sourced from P1's shared cells: labels vs labels_anchor per budget): anchored energy-gap improvement < 25% at EVERY budget => the anchor-value claim is retired.
- **KP4** (P3): zero-shot transfer displacement > 1.5x in-band OR any naive baseline (same naive set) wins on displacement at fine => the transfer claim is retired. Results in (1.25x, 1.5x] leave the claim alive in weakened, reported form only (gate (c) requires <= 1.25x); in either case (b) may still carry G2.
- **KP5** (P4): ANY falsification inequality violated => the theory-transcription claim is retired and the violation is investigated and published as a finding before any other Phase-2 claim is used.
- **KP6** (P5): rho_within < 0.3 => the alignment claim is retired (no gate impact).

## 5. Statistics floor

n_val >= 256 in-band and >= 256 at transfer; >= 3 seeds (0, 1, 2); per-seed AND per-instance arrays persisted in the report; t-statistics computed from stored arrays only. Aggregation rule: every gate and kill comparison is evaluated on seed-mean quantities (the transfer-ratio definition in Sec. 2 is an instance of this rule); per-seed and per-instance arrays exist for the record and for t-statistics, not for criterion evaluation. Definitions, for the avoidance of interpretive freedom: "x over the zero predictor" means (zero-predictor error) / (model error); parity in gate (b) and KP1 means disp_AR <= 1.10 x disp_labels@1024; energy-gap advantage in gate (b) and KP2 means 1 - egap_AR / egap_labels(b); anchored improvement in KP3 means 1 - egap_anchored(b) / egap_labels(b). Divergence rule (the E2 ar_ft lesson): runs that diverge (non-finite loss, or relative L2 displacement error exceeding 10) are reported per-seed and flagged; seed means always include them -- no silent exclusion. Precedence: thresholds and formulas stated in this document govern; for definitions not fixed here, the implementation at the stamped commit is authoritative; any prose-code divergence discovered later is a D-series deviation.
