# PRE-REGISTRATION CM2D -- the two-dimensional supervised networks retrained, with a stiffness-norm control

**Branch:** cmame-paper. **Status:** r2 DRAFT (7 October 2026), not stamped; the CONFIG_SHA256 line below is open. Stamping fills it before the run (Sec. 6); post-stamp criterion changes are prohibited. **Authority:** this English file governs. **Publication:** every outcome below is publishable; the manuscript for Computer Methods in Applied Mechanics and Engineering (CMAME; `paper/cmame/`) reports the run whatever its result.

## 1. Questions
Background. The manuscript's two-dimensional comparison is the pre-registered run of 16 July 2026 (`records/phase1/report_rec8_v2.json`, "July" below). It used the code of July 2026, whose decoder did not multiply the largest nodal load back onto the prediction. The label-free transformer -- trained on the assembled energy alone, without reference solutions (the code's amortised-Ritz regime, AR) -- was trained again with the current code in the base arm of the pre-registered experiment E1 (PREREG_E1; run of 29 September 2026, `records/wp8/e1/e1_2d_base/report.json`): seed-mean relative displacement error 0.0634 and relative energy gap 0.0383, against 0.166 and 0.0817 in July. The supervised networks were not retrained, so no two-dimensional comparison within the current code exists. The manuscript also explains the label-free network's lower stress errors by the norm of its objective: minimising the energy is supervised regression in the stiffness norm (its Corollary "Training objective"), whereas the supervised networks were trained on the relative Euclidean displacement error, which under-weights the stiff modes that carry the strains. When this file was first drafted, no supervised network had been trained with the stiffness norm in place of the Euclidean norm (since then, only in the small functional check of Sec. 4). July's labels+energy row added the energy (the squared stiffness norm of the error up to a constant, gradient-balanced) to the displacement loss and had a lower von Mises error than the labels-only transformer at 1,024 labels (0.257 against 0.433, July code), which the manuscript reads as a partial remedy; that row changed the objective in more than its norm, so the explanation has not been tested by a change of the norm alone at any scale beyond that check.

Notation. An instance has a stiffness matrix K (assembled, symmetric positive definite on the free degrees of freedom) and L = 4 load vectors F_j with reference solutions U*_j (K U*_j = F_j on the free degrees of freedom, zero on the constrained ones). The stiffness norm of a displacement vector v is ||v||_K = (v^T K v)^(1/2), with the constrained degrees of freedom set to zero. A network predicts u_j for load case j, zero on the constrained degrees of freedom (the decoder multiplies its output by the mask of the free ones); below, every product with K or F_j runs over the free degrees of freedom only, on which K is symmetric positive definite. The relative energy gap is g_j = ||u_j - U*_j||_K^2 / ||U*_j||_K^2. The supervised transformer of July minimised, per instance, L_D = (1/L) sum_j ||u_j - U*_j||_2 / ||U*_j||_2 (the relative Euclidean displacement error over the free degrees of freedom). Its stiffness-norm counterpart replaces the norm and nothing else: L_K = (1/L) sum_j ||u_j - U*_j||_K / ||U*_j||_K = (1/L) sum_j g_j^(1/2) (`SupervisedConfig.loss = "knorm"`). The label-free objective is (1/L) sum_j [u_j^T K u_j / 2 - F_j^T u_j] = (1/L) sum_j ||u_j - U*_j||_K^2 / 2 + const: the same norm, squared and not normalised. Since ||u_j - U*_j||_K^2 = u_j^T K u_j - 2 F_j^T u_j + F_j^T U*_j, L_K uses each reference solution only through one number per load case, ||U*_j||_K^2 = F_j^T U*_j, and its gradient with respect to the free entries of u_j (zero on the constrained ones), (K u_j - F_j) / (L ||u_j - U*_j||_K ||U*_j||_K), is the label-free objective's, (K u_j - F_j) / L, weighted per load case.

- **Q1.** With the current code, on E1's corpus, split and seeds, is the label-free transformer's relative energy gap lower than that of the supervised transformer trained with L_D on 1,024 labelled instances? (July's answer, within the July code: 0.0817 against 0.418.)
- **Q2.** Does training the same supervised transformer with L_K instead of L_D, changing nothing else, lower (a) its relative energy gap and (b) its von Mises error at 1,024 labels? (a) is close to a check that the change of loss took effect, since L_K minimises the mean over load cases of g_j^(1/2); (b), the von Mises error, is a term of neither loss -- by the manuscript's Proposition "Energy gap and stress error" the stiffness norm of the error is its volume-integrated stress-error energy and bounds the volume-weighted von Mises error, so L_K penalises that bound and L_D does not -- and reads the element-wise error of the evaluation suite on the validation instances (Sec. 9); it is the test of the manuscript's explanation.
- **Q3.** How does the transformer trained with L_K compare with the label-free one, which trained on the same 1,024 instances? By the Notation, the two objectives weight the same residual differently per load case (and were trained with different learning rates); H3 compares two networks, not training with and without labels. A two-sided reading, not a test.

## 2. Arms
One configuration, `configs/cm2d_v1.json`, run once on the GPU machine that holds E1's states and corpus ("the box": the AutoDL instance with an NVIDIA RTX 5090 on which E1 ran). Everything is E1's 2D base configuration otherwise: `runs/data2d` (n = 30,000, manifest SHA-256 `3553396d183115b843512a35cba1a1e8448b9e83e10c1d1a5b02b235a7c79d26`), split n_val = 256 / seed 1, transformer dim 256 / depth 8 / heads 8 with load-summary and geometry channels, graph network width 256 / depth 8, TensorFloat-32 matrix products on, seeds 0-2. Each supervised network trains on the labelled pool prefix pool[:b] -- the first b of the 1,024 instances on which the label-free states were trained -- for 200 epochs, one instance (all four load cases) per step, AdamW (learning rate 1.5e-3, weight decay 1e-4), gradient-norm clipping at 1, linear warm-up over the first 5% of steps then cosine decay: the settings of July's supervised rows, unchanged and not tuned for L_K.

| Row | Budgets b (labels) | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| label-free (`ar`) | none (pool of 1,024 unlabelled instances) | **Not trained.** E1's base states `runs/e1_2d_base/e8_states/ar_p1024_s{0,1,2}.pt` (204,800 steps per seed, learning rate 1e-3, otherwise the schedule, weight decay and clipping above), each refused unless its SHA-256 is the one E1's report records, evaluated with this code (`e8.reuse_from`, `supervised_grid`). |
| labels-only, L_D (`labels`) | 16, 64, 256, 1,024 | The supervised transformer of July, retrained with the current code. |
| stiffness norm, L_K (`labels_knorm`) | 16, 64, 256, 1,024 | The same transformer and settings with L_K. |
| graph network (`mgn`) | 64, 1,024 | The supervised graph network with L_D, retrained with the current code. |
| naive rows | 16, 64, 256, 1,024 | Zero field, scale-aware polynomial, nearest-neighbour field (fitted on pool[:b]; no seeds). |

At a given seed every transformer starts from the same initial weights (the model is built right after seeding with the seed), the label-free states included, and at 1,024 instances the label-free, L_D and L_K transformers visit the instances in the same order in every epoch (each trainer draws its epoch permutation from a generator seeded with the seed; the label-free objective draws nothing else from it). The L_D and L_K units of a seed and budget differ only in the loss. No labels+energy row and no pretrain-then-fine-tune row (the latter would train the reused states). The configuration is generated (`scripts/make_cm2d_config.py`, from `configs/e1_2d_base.json`, refused unless its canonical SHA-256 is PREREG_E1's stamped `4dfdea42...`); a test regenerates it byte for byte and checks that it differs from E1's base only in `e8.ar_only` (false), `e8.include_anchor` (false), `e8.include_ar_ft` (false), `e8.include_knorm` (true), `e8.mgn_budgets` ([64, 1024]), `e8.reuse_from`, `out`, `prereg_file` and `_comment`. The run refuses to start unless the configuration equals E1's outside the keys that shape only the supervised grid and the bookkeeping keys (output path, pre-registration file and guard, comment, evaluation block).

## 3. Evaluation and measurements
E1's validation split only (256 instances, four load cases each); no other evaluation set. For every row, budget and seed, the frozen metric suite per instance: relative energy gap, relative L2 displacement error, relative L2 von Mises error (over the elements), peak von Mises error (the absolute difference of the predicted and the reference maximum von Mises stress over the reference maximum), critical-region recall (the share of the 10% of elements with the highest reference von Mises stress that are also among the predicted 10%), each the mean over the instance's four load cases. A seed's value is its mean over the 256 instances; a row's value is the mean of its three seeds' values. The decisions read the relative energy gap and the relative L2 von Mises error at 1,024 labels.

## 4. Hypotheses and verdicts
Noise guard (the E-series rule, PREREG_E1 r13, as in PREREG_W9 Sec. 6): for a new arm A and a reference B with three per-seed values each, rel = mean(A)/mean(B) - 1 and SE_rel = sqrt(s_A^2/3 + s_B^2/3) / mean(B) with sample standard deviations (n - 1); tau = max(10%, 2 x SE_rel). "Lower beyond the guard" means rel < -tau; "worse beyond the guard" means rel > tau. Each verdict is reported with rel, SE_rel, tau and the per-seed values; a NOT SUPPORTED verdict is reported as "worse" when A is worse beyond the guard. A non-finite per-seed value of B leaves the hypothesis NOT EVALUATED (B is checked first, so also when A has one too); a non-finite per-seed value of A alone is a divergence, NOT SUPPORTED (diverged). Only non-finite values count as divergence: the divergence flags of E8 (the code's comparison of training regimes; a displacement error above 10) are reported, not used.
- **H1 (Q1; primary).** A = the label-free row, B = L_D at 1,024 labels; the relative energy gap. **SUPPORTED** iff A is lower beyond the guard; otherwise **NOT SUPPORTED**.
- **H2a (Q2a; primary).** A = L_K, B = L_D at 1,024 labels; the relative energy gap. **SUPPORTED** iff A is lower beyond the guard; otherwise **NOT SUPPORTED**.
- **H2b (Q2b; primary).** A = L_K, B = L_D at 1,024 labels; the relative L2 von Mises error. **SUPPORTED** iff A is lower beyond the guard; otherwise **NOT SUPPORTED**.
- **H3 (Q3; a pre-registered reading, no verdict).** A = L_K at 1,024 labels, B = the label-free row; the relative energy gap. Reported as exactly one of (or NOT EVALUATED, next item): the stiffness-norm transformer lower beyond the guard; the label-free transformer lower beyond the guard (A worse beyond the guard); no difference shown (within the guard); the stiffness-norm transformer diverged. B is the fixed arm (E1's states), as in PREREG_W9; with the roles exchanged the two thresholds would be 1/(1 + tau) and 1/(1 - tau) instead of 1 - tau and 1 + tau (0.909 and 1.111 against 0.900 and 1.100 at tau = 10%; SE_rel would also be divided by the other arm's mean). The reading with the roles exchanged is reported beside H3 (no criterion).
- The label-free row must pass its reuse checks (Sec. 6); if it does not -- a non-finite relative energy gap or displacement error of it never passes -- H1 and H3 are NOT EVALUATED and H2a and H2b are still adjudicated. Three hypotheses, three separate claims, no multiplicity correction; H3 makes no claim. Beside each verdict and H3 (no criterion): the guard on per-seed medians over the instances, rel with a Welch 95% interval, and a 95% interval for rel from resampling the instances (2,000 resamples, the same instances for both arms and every seed, the seeds kept); a verdict whose direction the medians do not show is reported with that disagreement.
- **Properties of the rule** (`scripts/cm2d_sims.py`, `records/cmame/cm2d_sims.json`; log-normal per-seed values, three seeds per arm, 200,000 trials per cell). With equal seed spreads (coefficient of variation c in both arms) and no true difference, a new arm is "lower beyond the guard" in 0.4% / 4.3% / 5.7% / 5.6% of trials at c = 5% / 10% / 20% / 30%, and "worse beyond the guard" in 0.8% / 4.8% / 5.6% / 5.6%; with a 5% spread in one arm and 20% in the other, the noisier arm reads lower beyond the guard in 9.4-9.8% of trials and higher in 5.1-5.3%. A true ratio of the arms' expected values of 0.8 is detected in 99.6% / 75% / 34% / 21% of trials at equal spreads of 5% / 10% / 20% / 30%, 0.7 in 100% / 98% / 60% / 36%, 0.5 in 100% / 100% / 97% / 77%. With the reference at July's L_D spread (8.9% in the relative energy gap; 7.1% in the von Mises error, H2b's reference), a new arm without a true difference reads lower beyond the guard in 2.3% / 3.9% / 8.1% / 11.2% / 15.8% of trials at new-arm spreads of 5% / 9% / 20% / 30% / 50% (a noisy arm's low draws come with small sample deviations), and a true ratio of 0.8 is detected in 91% / 82% / 54% / 43% / 37%; L_K's seed spread at this scale has never been measured: at new-arm spreads of 20-50% H2a's and H2b's false-support rate is 8-16%. For H3, without a true difference, a reading beyond the guard in either direction occurs in 1.3% / 9.1% / 11.3% / 11.2% of trials at equal spreads (about 15% with spreads of 5% and 20%). The model has no per-instance outliers: one validation instance at 20-40 times an arm's level moves a seed's mean by 7-15%, and with about one such instance per seed in one arm only (and 5% seed spreads otherwise), the other arm reads lower beyond the guard in 27-29% of trials (in about 5% when both arms have them). Since rel >= -1, "lower" cannot be shown once SE_rel >= 0.5, which one reference seed at four times the other two produces: a reference seed that blows up to a large finite value gives NOT SUPPORTED, not NOT EVALUATED. For scale: E1's label-free relative energy gaps have a coefficient of variation (sample standard deviation over mean) of 4.9% across seeds (0.0374 / 0.0405 / 0.0371; the spread comes from validation instance 233 in seed 1), July's L_D transformer at 1,024 labels 8.9%, and in July the label-free and L_D values at 1,024 labels stood in the ratio 0.20. The effects of L_K have not been measured at this scale.
- **Seen before the stamp.** A functional check of the code on 7 October 2026, after the first draft of this file and before its stamp (`scripts/cm2d_funccheck.py`, `records/cmame/cm2d_funccheck.json`), trained a transformer of width 32 and depth 2 for 25 epochs on 48 instances drawn by the 2D generator with its own seed (not E1's corpus), with L_D, with L_K and with the label-free objective, seeds 0 and 1, and evaluated it on 24 others: relative energy gap 0.259 / 0.287 with L_K against 12.7 / 9.58 with L_D, von Mises error 0.404 / 0.430 against 2.70 / 2.38 (the label-free objective: 0.451 / 0.250 and 0.532 / 0.407). Both reductions that H2a and H2b test were thus seen before the stamp, at a far smaller scale and on another corpus, where L_D's predictions were further from the solutions than the zero field (unlike July's L_D at 1,024 labels, 0.418); the same numbers show the label-free objective's gap below L_D's (H1's comparison) and L_K's against it differing in direction between the two seeds (H3's). This run tests them at E1's scale, on E1's corpus and split. No other training with L_K that bears on the hypotheses was run before the stamp: the tests train miniatures of a few synthetic instances for one to three epochs, and the end-to-end rehearsal of RUNBOOK_CMAME Sec. B (cmame-paper Stage 3; synthetic instances, a transformer of width 16 and depth 1 trained for three supervised epochs beside label-free states of one epoch) exercised the mechanics; its adjudication read H2a and H2b as SUPPORTED, H1 as NOT SUPPORTED and H3 as the stiffness-norm transformer lower.
- **What a verdict licenses.** SUPPORTED: on these seeds and validation instances, with this model, corpus, budget and code, A's value is lower than B's beyond tau. NOT SUPPORTED: no such reduction was shown; it is not evidence that the effect is absent or small (a reduction smaller than tau cannot be shown by design). H3's "no difference shown" is not equivalence.

## 5. Secondary readings (reported, no verdict; exploratory)
- Every metric of Sec. 3 at every budget for every row, per seed and seed-averaged; 80 comparisons with the Sec. 4 guard computed and stated, 16 per metric: at every budget L_D and L_K against the label-free row (B) and L_K against L_D (B), and at 64 and 1,024 labels the graph network against the label-free row and against L_D. Exploratory, not tests: without a true difference a comparison crosses the guard in a given direction in about 4-10% of cases at seed spreads between 5% and 20% (Sec. 4), so several crossings are expected by chance. For critical-region recall higher is better; each comparison records which direction is better.
- Label efficiency: per budget, the label-free advantage 1 - g_label-free / g_X for X = L_D and L_K (seed means of the relative energy gap); and for each of X = L_D and L_K and each of the relative energy gap and the displacement error separately, the smallest budget, if any, at which X's seed mean is below the label-free row's.
- Instance pairs at 1,024 labels (the manuscript's reading of July's run): on how many of the 768 instance-seed pairs one network has the lower relative energy gap, displacement error and von Mises error, for the label-free row against L_D, the label-free row against L_K, L_K against L_D, and the graph network against L_D.
- Worse than the zero field (the manuscript's Corollary "Zero-field test and ranking"): per row and budget, the instance-seed values with a relative energy gap above 1.
- The graph network at 1,024 labels: whether its seed-mean displacement error is the lowest of the four rows (label-free, L_D, L_K, graph network), and whether its relative energy gap is above L_D's and above the label-free row's. Neither earlier run showed the lowest displacement error together with an energy gap above L_D's: in July's 2D run its displacement error (0.167) was the highest of the trained rows and its relative energy gap (2.57) above L_D's (0.418); in the 3D run its displacement error (0.0168) was the lowest and its relative energy gap (0.447) above the label-free row's (0.0091) but below L_D's (31.2).
- E8's built-in readings as the run computes them (its kill conditions K2 and C1-advantage and its gate G1' come from earlier pre-registrations): reported, not criteria of this file.
- The L_D row against July's (other code; descriptive only).

## 6. Execution mechanics (exact)
- Stamping, after approval: `scripts/stamp_prereg_cm2d.py --date "<D Month YYYY>"` refuses a file whose status line, CONFIG_SHA256 line or last line is not this r2 draft's, and any configuration other than the generator's output; it fills CONFIG_SHA256[cm2d_v1] with the configuration's canonical SHA-256 (as `fejepa prereg --stamp`), the status line with the date, and the last line with this file's SHA-256, computed over the file with that line reading `PREREG_CM2D_SHA256 = <record after commit>`; then it reads the result back and verifies it. Commit; tag `prereg-cm2d` (no other tag on that commit); push the branch and the tag; the remote tag is verified before the run. The branch is frozen until the run has returned.
- The run (`RUNBOOK_CMAME.md` Sec. B), with nothing else on the GPU. Before it, `scripts/cm2d_precheck.py` must print GO: nothing else on the GPU and no other run (no process of this code or of its scripts), the package the run imports is this checkout's, E1's torch version with CUDA, 7 GB free before the run (5 GB before a restart); the checkout is `prereg-cm2d` (`git describe --tags --match prereg-cm2d` prints exactly that, and so does `git describe --always --dirty --tags`, which the report records) without local changes; the configuration is the generator's; its dry run verifies this file's stamp and E1's three states and plans a labelled pool prefix of 1,024; the corpus is E1's (manifest SHA-256) and its 256 validation and first 1,024 pool instances carry labels; E1's three states, evaluated on the validation split as the run evaluates them, reproduce E1's per-instance relative energy gaps and displacement errors (largest relative deviation at most 1e-4); one epoch of L_K training on two pool instances on the GPU gives finite values (these two checks use the GPU and are not run when it is busy); and no earlier attempt exists under `runs/cm2d/` (no report, `e8_states/`, run log or status file). Then one `run-config` of `configs/cm2d_v1.json` with the configuration's three workers and activation checkpointing on (the default, as for E1; it recomputes the same operations); the run starts only on that GO, which it uses up. - Adjudication: `scripts/adjudicate_cm2d.py`, from the return (its report, status file, provenance file and run logs). It records as deviations: restarts, units taken from the cache or resumed, more or fewer than one attempt or a non-zero exit (the status file's start and exit lines and the run logs), workers other than 3, activation checkpointing switched off, a non-zero solve ledger, a torch version or GPU other than E1's, and adjudicating code that differs from its `prereg-cm2d` version. Secondary readings never block a verdict (one that fails is recorded as not evaluated). The verdict file records every input's SHA-256 and the adjudicating code's.

- The new paths are tested (`tests/test_cm2d_training.py`): L_K equals the mean square root of the per-load relative energy gap and has its analytic gradient (double precision), and the loss the trainer computes equals it up to float32 round-off; training on it lowers it; the L_K and L_D units of a seed and budget differ only in the loss, and the label-free and supervised transformers of a seed share their initial weights and instance order; a run that reuses label-free states trains the supervised grid beside them, evaluates the reused states to the same per-instance values as the run that trained them (CPU), keeps the largest-budget states of the three trained rows, counts its planned steps exactly, and refuses a fine-tuning row, a missing `supervised_grid` flag, and a change to any key outside those that shape only the supervised grid, none of which changes the label-free units.
- Cost: 2,284,800 supervised steps (3 seeds x 200 epochs x (2 x 1,360 + 1,088) instances), 30 units on three workers. At the 52 ms per step measured on the box for this transformer's supervised training with activation checkpointing (`records/wp8/posthoc/profile_2d_head.json`; three concurrent label-free units took 53.5 ms per step each), plus a minute per unit, a simulation of the unit schedule gives 11.4 h if the graph network's step is as long as the transformer's (not measured: 9.9 / 14.4 / 17.9 h at a half / one and a half / twice as long; `records/cmame/cm2d_sims.json`); the L_K step (one sparse product with K per step, as in the label-free objective) was not timed on the box (on a CPU, in the functional check of Sec. 4, an L_K training took as long as an L_D one). ## 9. What this run does not test
- L_K uses L_D's settings (learning rate, schedule, epochs, gradient clipping), not tuned for it: a NOT SUPPORTED H2a or H2b does not show that no stiffness-norm loss would do better.
- H2a reads the mean of g_j, whose per-load square roots L_K averages (on the training instances); it checks that the change of loss reaches the validation energy gap. H2b, the von Mises error, is the reading of the manuscript's explanation; it uses the element-wise relative L2 error of the evaluation suite, not the volume-weighted error that the manuscript's Proposition "Energy gap and stress error" bounds.
- H1 compares two training recipes, as July did: the label-free states were trained with learning rate 1e-3 (the supervised rows 1.5e-3) in E1's run of 29 September 2026, and their values (Sec. 1) were known when this file was written; only the supervised rows are new. H3 compares two weightings of the same residual (Sec. 1, Notation) at those two learning rates, not one ingredient.
- The decisions read E1's 256 validation instances, on which the July and E1 results that motivated H1 and H2 were observed; there is no fresh evaluation set (PREREG_W9 adds one, an in-band holdout, for its own question).
- H2a's and H2b's reductions were seen before the stamp in the functional check of Sec. 4, at a far smaller scale and on another corpus; the run tests whether they hold at E1's scale.
- One corpus, one model size, two dimensions, no out-of-distribution set.
